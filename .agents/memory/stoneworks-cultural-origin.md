---
name: Stoneworks cultural origin
description: The family heritage and artistic foundation that should guide Stoneworks brand storytelling.
---

Stoneworks is rooted in a family from Bandha in Allahabad, Uttar Pradesh, India. Through generations of migration, the family carried culture and introduced the next generation to music, singing, painting, color, and art in every form. This inherited, multidisciplinary relationship with art is a core source of the brand's identity and should be reflected in its storytelling and work.

**Why:** The brand's distinctive point of view should come from lived family culture and migration, not only from external Italian interior-design references.

**How to apply:** Frame Stoneworks as a Karachi-based material and objects studio where memory, color, music, and art take form through marble, onyx, and spaces with feeling.
