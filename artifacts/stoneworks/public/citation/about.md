## Origin

St Werkz is rooted in a family from Bandha in Allahabad, Uttar Pradesh. Through generations of migration the family carried music, painting, colour, and art; that inherited relationship with making now takes form in marble, onyx, and travertine in Karachi.

Ancestral place: Bandha, Allahabad, Uttar Pradesh, India. Present practice: Karachi, Pakistan.

## How the studio works

1. **Listen first** — The room, the light, and what the surface should feel like — before a catalogue page.
2. **Find the right cut** — Tone, grain, scale, edge, and how the stone will age, not only the obvious face.
3. **A first range** — A studio estimate is an indicative band, never a final price or an invoice.
4. **Make it real** — Samples, honest guidance, and a path from conversation to installation.
