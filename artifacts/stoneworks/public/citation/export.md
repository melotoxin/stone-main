## Stone families discussed as lots

### Figured marble

High-polish stone read by vein and contrast — including noir-gold and Nero-type white calcite. Named as figured marble, not as a foreign quarry.

### Onyx

Translucent, banded stone. Backlight is reserved for slabs that allow it. Lots are discussed as faces first, lighting second.

### Travertine

Usually specified honed, with natural pits left open rather than filled to a glass face. Linear bedding for counters, tops and architectural edges.


## How a first lot starts

01. **Name the stone and the port** — Write which family you need — figured marble, onyx or travertine — the finish, and the destination port. Syed Rashid Ali takes the note.
02. **Read the photographed lot** — The studio replies with faces from the yard, not a generic catalogue. If the stone is not in hand, that is said plainly.
03. **Ship a sample crate** — A small crate of the agreed faces travels first. Feedback on shade and finish is collected before any container is discussed as a repeat.
04. **Then a first lot** — A first paid lot is usually talked through as a mixed twenty-foot load of photographed slabs, FOB Karachi. Larger cadence waits on that arrival.

## Commercial terms

### Incoterms

Conversations start FOB Karachi unless another term is agreed in writing. Freight, insurance and destination charges are named separately.

### Price

There is no published price list and no checkout. A range may be given for a named lot. It is not an invoice and not a bank quote.

### Quantity

A sample crate comes first. A mixed twenty-foot load is a typical first conversation, not a minimum advertised to the internet.

### What we will not claim

Italian origin, awards, identical next-container colour without lotting, or a volume we have not yet shipped together.


## Quality checklist (named before packing)

### Shade lotting

Faces that will travel together are grouped by eye from the yard photographs, then confirmed before packing.

### Finish

Honed, polished, or backlight on onyx only. The finish is named on the lot, not assumed from a moodboard.

### Measure

Thickness, calibration and count are stated per lot in writing. They are not printed as a standing catalogue.

### Fill and resin

What is filled, and what is left open — especially on travertine — is said before the crate leaves.

### Packing

Edge protection, crate method and loading can be filmed from the yard for a serious buyer. A packing film is made for that conversation; it is not a stock clip.

### Refusal

Unnamed mixed lots sold as one shade, quarry names we cannot stand behind, and stone we have not photographed will not be offered.
